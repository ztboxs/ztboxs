## Building AI products across software and hardware

Hi, I'm **ztboxs**. With a background in full-stack software development, I now focus on **AI applications and products that bring software and hardware together**.

I'm interested in more than making individual features work: how can AI, applications, and devices come together as a useful product?

<a id="recent-work"></a>
## What I've been working on over the past year

### AI applications & intelligent devices

My focus has gradually expanded from software development to products that connect AI with physical devices. I'm exploring voice, vision, and natural interaction, with an interest in how AI can help in real-world situations beyond a conversation on a screen.

### From requirements to a complete system

My work spans product requirements, system architecture, mobile and backend development, device integration, deployment, and end-to-end testing. Rather than staying within one layer, I focus on how the parts work together and how an idea develops from a prototype into a usable experience.

### AI-assisted development

I'm also incorporating AI coding tools and agent collaboration into everyday development, exploring task decomposition, coordination across system components, and verification. The goal is not just to generate code, but to make complex product development more organized and its results easier to verify.

<p>
  <sub>Most of my current work is in closed-source products.</sub>
</p>

<a id="approach"></a>
## How I approach the work

Start with a concrete problem, validate the need, then choose the technology. Pay attention to the user experience as well as reliability, maintainability, and room to iterate.
